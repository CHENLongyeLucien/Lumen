Let Lumen open your local videos

Chrome blocks extensions from reading files on your computer by default. Turn on one setting and Lumen will open your local videos automatically.

Checking…
  1. Click Open extension settings below (or go to chrome://extensions and click Details under Lumen).
  2. Turn on Allow access to file URLs.
  3. Open your video again. Chrome reloads Lumen when this setting changes, so this tab may close.

You can also drag and drop a video onto the player page: this works without the setting.

Good to know

  • Lumen uses Chrome's codecs: MP4 (H.264/AAC) and WebM (VP8/VP9/AV1) always work; MKV, AVI and H.265 depend on your machine.
  • Only direct video files are handled: .mp4 .m4v .webm .ogv .ogg .mov. Streaming sites (YouTube, Netflix…) are not.
  • If another extension also redirects video links, the two may conflict.
  • You can turn the redirect off at any time by clicking the Lumen icon in the toolbar.
